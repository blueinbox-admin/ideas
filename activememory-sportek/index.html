Guardrails (what reps must never say)

guardrailNever describe a fabric as "chlorine-proof." Our chlorine-resist styles are chlorine-resistant with extended life.
✓
guardrailNever tell a customer to sublimate on a non-PFP nylon-spandex fabric; it will not hold the print.

guardrailNever quote stock or a roll length as guaranteed without checking the live catalog.
